# Market Size & Digital Readiness of Egypt's Small/Medium Retail Market (for a cloud POS + inventory + accounting SaaS)

Research date: 2026-11-19. All figures carry unit, year and source. "Inference" = our own arithmetic or judgement, not a published number.

## 1. How many retail establishments / SMEs exist in Egypt (census, MSMEDA, World Bank, OECD)? Retail vs wholesale, governorate, formal vs informal

### Takeaway
The CAPMAS Sixth Economic Census (reference year 2022/2023, published Oct 2025) counts **3.858 million establishments**, of which **54.4% are in wholesale & retail trade**, i.e. roughly **2.1 million trade establishments** (our arithmetic). About **half of private establishments are informal (50.7%)**, and the vast majority are micro (fewer than 5 workers). A clean retail-only vs wholesale-only split and a per-governorate trade count were not found in accessible public summaries.

### Cited Findings
- Sixth Economic Census 2022/2023: total establishments **3.858 million**, up **3.1%** from **3.743 million** in the 2017/2018 census — [Shorouk News, 27 Oct 2025, quoting CAPMAS](https://www.shorouknews.com/mobile/news/view.aspx?cdate=27102025&id=0f05cb23-fcde-4697-87ac-c0c42c3f9744)
- **Wholesale and retail trade = 54.4% of all establishments** (largest sector); manufacturing 13.4%; food & accommodation services (restaurants, hotels, cafés) 4.8%; all other activities 27.4% — [Shorouk News / CAPMAS](https://www.shorouknews.com/mobile/news/view.aspx?cdate=27102025&id=0f05cb23-fcde-4697-87ac-c0c42c3f9744)

- Fifth census (2017/18): **3.7 million establishments**, **89% with fewer than 5 employees** — [OECD, SME and Entrepreneurship Policy in Egypt (2025)](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/sme-and-entrepreneurship-policy-in-egypt_195d9c43-en/full-report/sme-and-entrepreneurship-performance-and-characteristics-in-egypt_a963c2a5.html)
- 2017/18: "More than half of private establishments work in wholesale and retail trade with **more than 2 million** establishments" — [World Bank country document (2023)](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/52f41abeaf1cad6197386a5b9cf22f5c-0350022023/original/EGYPT.pdf)
- 2017/18 census sampling frame: **4,119,716** establishments — [ERF Data Portal, ECOCENS 2017/2018](https://www.erfdataportal.com/index.php/catalog/239)
- CAPMAS metadata catalogue for the 2022/2023 census (microdata/tables may be requestable) — [CAPMAS metadata](https://metadata.capmas.gov.eg/en/index.php/catalog/821)
- Retail & trade establishments are mostly small, low capital, low value-added — [Zawia3](https://zawia3.com/en/6-economic-census/)

### Inferences
- **Trade establishments 2022/23 ≈ 0.544 × 3.858M ≈ 2.10 million** (wholesale + retail combined, formal + informal). Consistent with the World Bank's ">2 million" for 2017/18.
- If trade follows the private-sector average formality share (49.3%) — an assumption, trade may be more informal — **formal trade establishments ≈ 1.0 million**.
- Food & accommodation (cafés, restaurants) ≈ 0.048 × 3.858M ≈ **185,000** establishments — relevant for coffee-shop/café use cases but outside "retail trade".
- Given 89% of all establishments had <5 workers (2017/18), the vast majority of the 2.1M are kiosks/micro shops (one owner, 1–2 helpers). The realistic serviceable segment is the minority with ≥2 staff, meaningful SKU counts, or credit sales (wholesalers, minimarkets, mobile shops, roasters/spice shops).
- Illustrative sizing (our assumptions, to be refined in the final report):
  - **TAM** (all ~2.1M trade establishments × EGP 499/month entry price × 12) ≈ **EGP 12.6 bn/yr ≈ USD 235M/yr** at ~53.7 EGP/USD. This is an upper bound; most micro kiosks will never pay.
  - **SAM** (formal, urban, ≥2 workers; assume ~15–25% of trade establishments ≈ 300k–525k shops) × ~EGP 800/month blended ≈ **EGP 2.9–5.0 bn/yr (USD 54–94M)**. The 15–25% is an assumption, not a published figure.
  - **SOM** (1–2% of SAM in 3–5 years ≈ 3,000–10,000 paying shops) ≈ **EGP 29–100M/yr**. Assumption-driven.

### Gaps
- No public split of the 54.4% into **retail vs wholesale**, nor sub-activity counts (grocery, mobile phones, clothing, pharmacies). The full CAPMAS census tables (ISIC 4-digit) would contain this; the CAPMAS bulletin was not reachable in this session.
- No **per-governorate count of trade establishments** found in public summaries for 2022/23.
- Size-class distribution (<5 workers share) for the 2022/23 census was not found; the 89% figure is from 2017/18.
- MSMEDA's own count of MSMEs and any ILO informal-retail estimate were not located in this pass.
- Pharmacies (separately regulated) and van-sales wholesalers could not be isolated.

## 2. What share of shops use POS/accounting software vs paper/Excel?

### Takeaway
No credible Egypt-specific survey measuring POS/accounting software adoption among retail shops was found. The closest proxies are (a) Visa/MSMEDA 2025: 55% of cash-only merchants intend to acquire POS systems, and (b) the OECD citing an older MCIT/CAPMAS ICT survey where over half of surveyed (mostly larger, formal) enterprises use computers for accounting. Small-shop software penetration is very likely low, but it is not quantified.

### Cited Findings
- Visa "Value of Acceptance" study with MSMEDA (Jul 2025; 270 face-to-face interviews with SME owners/managers by 4Sight Research): **53%** of merchants surveyed implemented digital payments in the last two years; **55% of cash-only merchants** interested in adopting / intend to acquire POS systems; **80%** satisfied with card acceptance; **77%** say digital payments are crucial for growth; **59%** newly embracing online payments; **53%** accept payments via social media; **41%** worried about cash fraud/robbery; SMEs want loyalty programmes, data insights, and simple tools — [Visa newsroom, 28 Jul 2025](https://www.visa.com/en-eg/newsroom/egypt-smes-digital-payments-growth-study)
- MSMEDA's CEO, in the same release, links the partnership to e-payments and **e-invoices** and to the tax incentives of MSME Law 152/2020 and Law 6/2025 — [Visa newsroom](https://www.visa.com/en-eg/newsroom/egypt-smes-digital-payments-growth-study)
- OECD (Jun 2025), citing the MCIT/CAPMAS ICT usage survey 2019/2020: **over 70%** of surveyed enterprises use computers for internet and office work; **more than half** use computers for process automation and **financial management and accounting**; e-commerce **<2%** of total sales; social media use about 70% of firms with >250 employees vs just over 40% of firms with 10–49 employees — [OECD, SME digitalisation in Egypt](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/sme-and-entrepreneurship-policy-in-egypt_195d9c43-en/full-report/sme-digitalisation-in-egypt_16206c32.html)

### Inferences
- The OECD/MCIT figures are skewed toward formal, larger firms (2019/20) and cannot be read as "half of shops use accounting software". For micro retail, adoption is plausibly in the low single digits to low teens percent. **This is an unsourced estimate.**
- The Visa sample (n=270) is small and SME-focused; its 55% "intend to acquire POS" is about **payment terminals**, not management software, but it shows openness to digital tools.

### Gaps
- No survey found from Paymob, Fawry, CBE, Rabbit, Vondera, or consulting firms giving the share of Egyptian shops using POS/inventory/accounting software vs paper/Excel. Primary research (owner interviews or a short field survey) is recommended to fill this.
- No data on how many shops already use local desktop POS (pirated or one-time licence) vs cloud.

## 3. Digital readiness: smartphones/internet, card/InstaPay/wallet acceptance, 2023–2026 trends

### Takeaway
Consumer and payment-side readiness is high and rising fast: ~82% internet penetration (early 2025), 79% financial inclusion (Jun 2026), 24M+ InstaPay users and 67M mobile wallets (Sep 2026), 1.35M+ POS terminals. Merchant-side digitization lags but is being pushed by regulation (Soft POS/Tap-to-Phone from Feb 2026; e-receipt compliance tied to the simplified tax regime).

### Cited Findings
- **Internet users: 96.3 million (81.9% penetration), Jan 2025**, up **12 million (+14.6%)** y/y; **mobile connections 116 million (99.0% of population)**; **93.7%** of connections are broadband (3G/4G/5G) — [DataReportal, Digital 2025: Egypt](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-egypt)
- 98.2 million internet users at end-2025 (82.7%) attributed to DataReportal — [secondary social post](https://www.facebook.com/TontivaInc/posts/egypt-had-982-million-internet-users-at-the-end-of-2025-827-of-the-population-da/122164397925352723/) (not verified against the DataReportal 2026 report; treat as unconfirmed)
- **Financial inclusion: 79% at end-June 2026 = 56.4 million of 71.4 million adults (15+)**; 77.6% at end-2025; 76.3% June 2025; 74.8% Dec 2024; women 72.5%; youth (15–35) 58% — [Ahram Online, 6 Aug 2026, citing CBE](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/574144.aspx); June 2025 release: [CBE](https://www.cbe.org.eg/en/news-publications/news/2025/09/03/09/57/financial-inclusion-rates-egypt-as-of-june-2025) (inclusion +214% between 2016 and June 2025)
- **InstaPay / IPN: 24+ million registered users and 2.4+ billion transactions worth ~EGP 7 trillion cumulatively through Sep 2026**; **mobile wallets: 67 million**, transactions >EGP 4 trn through Sep 2026; internet/mobile banking ~EGP 13 trn; IPN launched Mar 2022; **QR payments added June 2024**; InstaPay had >16 million users by June 2025 (≈8M users added in ~1 year) — [Ahram Online, 8 Nov 2026, quoting CBE Deputy Governor](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/581997.aspx)
- InstaPay registered users ~**12.5 million** at end-Dec 2024 — [CBE, 30 Dec 2024](https://www.cbe.org.eg/en/news-publications/news/2024/12/30/12/31/cbe-extending-the-exemption-of-individuals-from-instapay-for-a-renewable-period-of-3-months?utm)
- IPN Q2 2026: **1.47 bn transactions worth EGP 4.33 trn**, ~22M registered customers; InstaPay transfer fees started April 2025 — [Norbr FAQ](https://www.norbr.com/faq/what-is-instapay-in-egypt); related Ahram headline linked from [Ahram 581997](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/581997.aspx)
- Payment-acceptance infrastructure (vendor blog, Jul 2026, citing CBE/NTRA): **>1.35 million POS terminals**; **43.5+ million Meeza cards** (mid-2025); **46.3 million active telco wallets** (NTRA, Q2 2025); Fawry **382,600 merchant acceptance points** (date not stated) — [XPay blog](https://xpay.app/blog/payment-methods-egyptian-customers-prefer). Secondary source; verify against CBE statistical bulletin.
- **Soft POS / Tap-to-Phone**: CBE launched contactless acceptance on smartphones/tablets (Feb 2026), letting merchants accept cards without a terminal — [CBE infographic, 1 Feb 2026](https://www.cbe.org.eg/en/news-publications/news/2026/02/01/10/42/infograph-february-1-2026); [Fintech News](https://fintechnews.ae/30054/fintechegypt/egypt-contactless-smartphone-payments/)
- Mobile payments market: POS channels **48.11%** of Egypt mobile-payments market in 2025, 9.12% CAGR (Mordor estimate) — [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/egypt-mobile-payment-market)
- Regulatory push: benefiting from the **simplified tax regime (Law 6/2025) for businesses with annual turnover < EGP 20M** requires **full compliance with both e-invoice and e-receipt systems** (ETA head, Mar 2026) — [Daily News Egypt, 8 Mar 2026](https://dailynewsegypt.com/2026/03/08/e-invoice-e-receipt-compliance-required-to-benefit-from-simplified-tax-system-eta-head/)
- E-invoice mandatory rollout began Nov 2020; e-receipt (B2C) mandatory rollout began July 2022, phased from large taxpayers downward (3rd phase Nov 2022 ≈ 2,000 companies) — [dddinvoices](https://dddinvoices.com/learn/e-invoicing-in-egypt); [Daftra hub](https://www.daftra.com/en/hub/egypt-electronic-receipt)

### Inferences
- Shop owners and staff nearly universally have smartphones and mobile data; the bottleneck is **merchant behaviour and cost sensitivity, not connectivity**. This favours mobile-first / Android POS and offline-tolerant design.
- InstaPay QR and wallets give shops cheap digital acceptance; a POS SaaS that records InstaPay/wallet/cash/card tenders and reconciles them is aligned with the trend.
- The **e-receipt + simplified tax regime link** is the strongest "must-have" driver for formal small shops (turnover < EGP 20M). ETA e-receipt integration is likely a key selling feature for the formal segment.

### Gaps
- No official CBE count of **merchants** (as opposed to terminals) accepting cards/QR/InstaPay in 2025–2026 was found; the number of InstaPay QR merchants is not published in the sources reviewed.
- Number of taxpayers registered under Law 6/2025 and number of shops onboarded to e-receipt were not found.
- DataReportal 2026 Egypt figures were not verified directly.

## 4. Macro context for EGP SaaS pricing 2025–2026: inflation, EGP/USD, SME purchasing power, typical software spend

### Takeaway
Inflation has fallen from a 38% peak (Sep 2023) to about 12–13% (Oct 2026) but prices roughly doubled vs 2022, and real wages stay weak. The EGP is around 53–54 per USD in Nov 2026 (vs ~47.8 at end-Sep 2025). Pricing in EGP must allow periodic increases; no data on typical small-shop software spend was found.

### Cited Findings
- Urban headline inflation **12.7% in Oct 2026** (from 13.9% in Sep); all-time high **38.0% in Sep 2023**; Trading Economics forecasts ~10% in 2027, 8% in 2028 (model forecast) — [Trading Economics, citing CAPMAS](https://tradingeconomics.com/egypt/inflation-cpi)
- Core inflation **13.1% in Oct 2026** (from 14.1% in Sep) — [Egypt Today post](https://www.facebook.com/EgyptTodayMag/posts/egypts-annual-core-inflation-slowed-to-131-percent-in-october-2026-compared-to-1/1610622241080092/)
- Oct 2025: urban inflation 12.5%, core 12.1% — [Reuters, 10 Nov 2025](https://www.reuters.com/world/africa/egypts-annual-urban-consumer-inflation-quickens-125-october-2025-11-10/)
- "Egyptians pay twice what they paid in 2022" despite falling inflation — [Zawia3, 13 Nov 2026 (headline only, article not read)](https://zawia3.com/en/inflation-prices/); inflation fell from >35% in 2023 to ~12% by end-2025 without restoring purchasing power; real wages remain low — [Zawia3](https://zawia3.com/en/6-economic-census/)
- **EGP/USD ≈ 53.68** (Wise mid-market, Nov 2026); 90-day average 52.65, 30-day high 56.27 — [Wise](https://wise.com/us/currency-converter/usd-to-egp-rate/history)
- IMF programme reference rate **47.8077 EGP/USD** (CBE official buy rate, 30 Sep 2025) — [IMF Country Report 2026, 5th & 6th EFF reviews](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2026/english/1egyea2026001.pdf)
- GDP growth **4.4% in FY2024/25** (vs 2.4% prior year); FY2025/26 forecast ~4.2% (World Bank) — [Zawia3](https://zawia3.com/en/6-economic-census/)
- Unemployment **6% in Q3 2026** (Ahram headline) — [Ahram Online related list](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/581997.aspx)
- IMF staff-level agreement on final EFF/RSF reviews unlocking $2.3bn (Nov 2026, Ahram headline) — [Ahram Online related list](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/581997.aspx)

### Inferences
- Inflation of ~12–14% means an EGP list price loses about that much real value per year; plan an **annual EGP price review** (or USD-indexed list with EGP billing). The EGP weakened about 12% from ~47.8 (Sep 2025) to ~53.7 (Nov 2026).
- At 53.7 EGP/USD, the project's internal price points (EGP 499 / 999 / 1,499 per month, internal pricing decision of 2026-11-15, not an external source) equal about **USD 9.3 / 18.6 / 27.9 per month**, cheap by global SaaS standards but meaningful for a micro shop.

### Gaps
- No published data on **typical monthly software/IT spend of an Egyptian small shop**, nor small-retail average turnover or margins in 2025–2026. Competitor price points (Daftra, Foodics, Odoo partners, local POS vendors) are a proxy and are covered by the competitor research track.
- No source on SME willingness to pay for SaaS in Egypt.

## 5. Market-size estimates for Egypt / MENA retail POS, retail software, SME SaaS

### Takeaway
Only global POS software / cloud POS market estimates (2025–2026) were found from commercial research firms. No credible Egypt-specific POS-software or retail-SaaS market size was located; an Egypt figure has to be built bottom-up from establishment counts (Section 1).

### Cited Findings
- Global **cloud POS** market **USD 6.1 bn (2025)**, IMARC forecast USD 25.9 bn by 2034 — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/cloud-pos-market)
- Global **POS software** market **USD 16.05 bn (2025) → USD 18.04 bn (2026)**, forecast USD 32.33 bn (Mordor) — [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/pos-software-market)
- Global POS software **USD 15.98 bn (2026) → USD 22.63 bn by 2030**, 9.1% CAGR — [ResearchAndMarkets](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/5980344/point-sale-pos-software-market-report)
- Global POS software **USD 16.94 bn (2025)** → USD 58.52 bn by 2035 (MRFR) — [Market Research Future](https://www.marketresearchfuture.com/reports/pos-software-market-8384)
- Global POS (hardware + software) **USD 38.57 bn (2025), USD 43.32 bn (2026)**, 13.1% CAGR to 2034 — [Fortune Business Insights](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/point-of-sale-pos-market-106336)
- Egypt mobile-payments market: POS channel share 48.11% (2025) — [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/egypt-mobile-payment-market) (payments, not software)

### Inferences
- Commercial global estimates disagree by about 2.5x depending on scope (software-only vs hardware + software vs cloud-only). They are useful only as a trend signal (roughly 9–13% CAGR), not for sizing Egypt.
- A bottom-up Egypt TAM of roughly **USD 235M/yr** (Section 1 inference, at EGP 499/month × 2.1M establishments) would be about 1.3% of the global POS software market. That is plausible as an absolute upper bound, and confirms that the realistic SAM is much smaller.

### Gaps
- No Egypt- or MENA-specific POS software / retail SaaS / SME SaaS market size found (Mordor/IMARC Egypt-POS-software pages not located; MENA figures not found in this pass).
- No published count of cloud POS subscribers in Egypt, or competitor customer counts (e.g., Foodics/Daftra Egypt user numbers), in this research track.
