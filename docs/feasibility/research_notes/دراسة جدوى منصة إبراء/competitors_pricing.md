# Competitive landscape and pricing: cloud POS / retail inventory / SME accounting for Egyptian shops (research date 2026-11-19)

> Scope note: Ibraa (إبراء) reference points used for comparison: free plan (150 invoices/month, 100 items, 2 users); paid 499 / 999 / 1,499 EGP per month; yearly = 10 months; founder prices 349 / 699 / 1,099.
> FX caveat: no live FX source was fetched in this session. Where USD/SAR prices are converted, an **assumed** rate of about 50 EGP/USD and about 13 EGP/SAR is used and marked "(approx.)". The report writer should check the rate on the publication date.
> All prices were captured between Oct and Nov 2026 unless stated.

## Q1. Who actually sells to Egyptian retail shops in 2025-2026, and how do they price?

### Takeaway
The Egyptian market has four tiers. (1) Regional cloud ERP/accounting with Egypt pricing and ETA e-invoice: Daftra, Wafeq, Edara, Odoo via partners. (2) Global free or cheap POS: Loyverse, Zoho Books' free plan. (3) A crowded field of small Egyptian cloud POS start-ups, many priced at **499-1,500 EGP/month**. One of them, Solo POS (restaurants), uses **exactly the 499 / 999 / 1,499 EGP price points that Ibraa plans**. (4) Payment companies that bundle POS terminals with light inventory: Geidea, Paymob, Fawry. I found no evidence that Saudi players Rewaa and Qoyod sell actively in Egypt. Foodics is present but targets F&B and does not publish Egypt prices.

### Inferences
- **Ibraa's price ladder sits at the low end of Egyptian cloud POS**, below Daftra's monthly list price (733 EGP) for the entry tier. A Solo POS customer would see identical numbers (499/999/1,499), so the price points read as "market standard" rather than disruptive. Differentiation must come from the free plan and retail-specific features, not from price alone.
- Daftra's Egypt price (733 EGP vs $30 list) implies purchasing-power pricing of roughly half the USD list. Regional players do localize for Egypt.
- Daftra Basic's 100 invoices/month cap is *lower* than Ibraa's free plan of 150 invoices/month. That is a strong marketing comparison.
- Daftra charges extra for branches, warehouses beyond 1-5, users and offline POS. "All-inclusive per shop" pricing is a positioning lever, and Managely also markets against Daftra's per-user fees ([managely](https://managely.cloud/ar/best-daftra-alternative-in-egypt)).
- Loyverse is the main "free" benchmark. It lacks Egyptian ETA compliance, an Arabic customer-debt (آجل) ledger focus, and vans. Its free multi-store is more generous than many paid local tools.

### Gaps
- Qoyod: no evidence of active Egypt sales or EGP pricing found (not searched deeply). Rewaa: only Saudi pricing found and no Egypt presence evidence.
- Foodics Egypt price: not public. The only figure is a third-party estimate.
- Loyverse "Advanced inventory" and "Unlimited history" add-on prices were not shown on the global page.
- Edara/إدارة, Sahl, Aronium, Vondera, Rabbit, Bosta merchant tools: not covered by direct pricing pages in this session. Aronium pricing and the Egypt reseller presence of Sahl/Vondera are unknown.

- Review-based weaknesses: Capterra shows Daftra at 3.0/5 from a single review ([Capterra alternatives](https://www.capterra.com/p/244865/Daftra/alternatives/)). There is not enough review data for meaningful weakness analysis. Google Play reviews were not collected.

## Q2. Typical one-time ("مدى الحياة") desktop POS licence prices in Egypt

### Takeaway
The one-time desktop alternative is real and broad. It ranges from **"free with hardware"** bundles and **$99-279 (≈5,000-14,000 EGP approx.)** supermarket programs up to **10,000-40,000+ EGP** for restaurant-grade perpetual licences. Annual "activation/support" fees often apply, so "lifetime" is not always truly one-time.

### Cited Findings
- Traditional on-premise POS licence fees run 10,000-30,000+ EGP one-time. Full hardware (touch device + printer + drawer) costs 7,500-22,000 EGP. Setup and training cost 500-2,000 EGP — [Talabxy cost guide](https://talabxy.com/ar/blog/pos-system-cost-egypt-2026) (Apr 2026)
- Lifetime licences for restaurants run "about 40,000 EGP" one-time, against cloud from ~15,000 EGP/yr — [DineCore](https://dinecore.wizzora.app/ar/blog/restaurant-pos-prices-egypt) (Oct 2026)
- Nibras POS sells a permanent per-branch licence, offline, with a manager app, "starting from 15,000 EGP" — [Nibras](https://nibras-media.com/store/c/pos/)
- MasterSoft supermarket software (scale barcode support) sells a perpetual licence from $99. "Sales" edition costs $279 one-time, and annual activation costs $150/year — [mastersofteg.com](https://mastersofteg.com/industries/supermarkets/software)
- Hardware dealers on Facebook sell "complete cashier device" bundles that come "with free lifetime system" software, plus lifetime warranty on software and 18 months on hardware — [Facebook dealer post](https://www.facebook.com/groups/362230532404868/posts/1419673873327190/); [Facebook video ad](https://www.facebook.com/montaser01018212181/videos/1525356912947972/)
- Free open-source Arabic cashier programs circulate on YouTube ("Sales System") — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WYf9JARWAag)
- ETA-approved e-receipt POS devices cost 4,500-18,000 EGP per device in 2026 — [Pioneers Solutions](https://pioneers-solutions.com/blog-electronic-receipt-program-retail-stores)

### Inferences
- For a small shop, a 15,000 EGP one-time licence equals about 30 months of Ibraa Starter at 499, or 43 months at the 349 founder price. The free plan plus a low entry tier is credible against one-time licences *if* offline reliability matches desktop.
- The hardware-bundle "free software" model means dealers are a distribution channel. They could be partners (reseller margin) rather than only competitors.

### Gaps
- No OLX/Dubizzle/Jumia listing prices were collected. Specific prices for "برنامج كاشير" from local vendors, e.g. Aronium resellers, remain unverified.

## Q3. Regulatory feature pressure: ETA e-invoice / e-receipt

### Takeaway
E-receipt (B2C) integration is becoming a must-have for retail POS in Egypt. The ETA maintains a list of approved POS vendors. Ibraa's competitors mostly claim e-invoice (B2B) but rarely e-receipt.

### Cited Findings
- The ETA runs an e-receipt portal with POS registration, an SDK, and a published list of approved POS supplier companies (v29, Sep 2026) — [eta.gov.eg e-receipt services](https://eta.gov.eg/ar/content/e-receipt-services)
- A vendor guide claims Decision 281/2025 extended e-receipt to retail and consumer transactions from 1 Jan 2026 and lowered the VAT/invoicing registration threshold to 250,000 EGP revenue. It also says receipts must be submitted within 72 hours with a QR code — [menubarcode guide](https://menubarcode.com/guides/eta-einvoicing). **Unverified vendor claim; check against ETA decisions.**
- The ETA offers simplified tax treatment for businesses under 20M EGP annual revenue — [ETA Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/tax.egypt/videos/1360004005576210/)
- E-receipt was not listed for Daftra, Zoho, Wafeq or Edara on public pages. Dopave says "not yet" — [Dopave](https://www.dopave.net/blog/best-accounting-software-egypt)

### Inferences
- Being on the ETA approved-vendor list, or having a clear e-receipt roadmap, would be a strong differentiator against both Loyverse/Zoho (no ETA) and one-time desktop programs (often not integrated).

### Gaps
- The exact mandate scope and dates for small retailers in 2026 were not verified from a primary ETA decision text.

## Q4. Gaps and opportunities for a new entrant (Ibraa)

### Takeaway
No surveyed product combines all of these: a permanent free plan with real limits, Egyptian-Arabic retail UX, a customer-debt (آجل) ledger, weight-barcode items, delivery vans and Android + Windows offline apps at ≤1,499 EGP. Each competitor covers only part of it. Daftra is broad but metered and accounting-heavy. Loyverse is free but not Egyptian. Local POS tools are mostly restaurant-focused or desktop-only.

### Cited Findings
- Restaurant/F&B focus dominates the Egyptian cloud POS content and vendors: Foodics, Solo POS, DineCore, Talabxy, Nibras — [Solo POS](https://www.getsolopos.com/en/blog/pos-price-egypt); [DineCore](https://dinecore.wizzora.app/ar/blog/restaurant-pos-prices-egypt); [Nibras](https://nibras-media.com/store/c/pos/)
- Weight-scale barcode is advertised mainly by desktop supermarket programs (MasterSoft) and some cloud tools (Namra) — [MasterSoft](https://mastersofteg.com/industries/supermarkets/software); [Namra](https://www.getnamra.com/blog/best-cashier-software-supermarket-restaurant)
- Daftra meters invoices (100/mo on Basic), warehouses, branches, users and offline POS — [daftra.com/plans](https://www.daftra.com/plans)
- Annual-only billing (Rewaa) is an upfront cash barrier for small shops — [Snad](https://www.snad.io/en/blog/afdal-barnamaj-nuqat-bay-mahalat-tajzia-saudi)

### Inferences
- **Retail-first positioning** (grocery, butcher/مجزر, spices/عطارة, household goods, wholesale with vans) is under-served compared with F&B.
- **Free plan vs Daftra Basic**: 150 free invoices against 100 paid invoices is an easy headline comparison.
- **Vans/route sales + آجل ledger** are not marketed by any surveyed competitor in Egypt. This is a niche for distributors and wholesalers.
- **Price risk**: 499/999/1,499 matches Solo POS exactly. Consider whether founder prices (349/699/1,099) should be the public anchor, or whether to reshape tiers around retail value metrics (branches/vans) to avoid looking like a copy.
- **Distribution**: hardware dealers and Facebook groups are where small shops buy. A reseller/dealer program and bundling with ETA-approved devices may matter more than self-signup alone.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data on market share of these vendors among Egyptian retail shops.
- No systematic review mining (Google Play / Facebook comments) on pain points such as support quality, offline sync failures or price hikes.
