# Delete Your Account and Data

**Version:** `{{LEGAL_VERSION}}`  
**Effective Date:** `{{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}`

---

## 1. Who can request deletion

- **Shop owner (workspace owner):** may request deletion of the whole shop account and all of its data.
- **A user inside a shop (cashier or staff):** asks the shop manager directly, or uses the form below and we forward the request to the shop.
- **A customer or supplier recorded by a shop:** the shop is the data controller, so the request goes to that shop; the platform gives the shop the tools to carry it out.

## 2. How to submit a request

1. Fill in the "Account deletion request" form on this page: name, mobile number, shop code (if you have one) and an optional email.
2. You get an on-screen confirmation that the request was recorded.
3. Our support team contacts you within `3` working days to verify your identity and your authority over the account before anything is deleted.
4. Once verified, deletion is completed within `30` days at most and we confirm the outcome.

You can also reach us directly at `{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}` or `{{SUPPORT_PHONE}}`.

## 3. Data that is deleted

- User accounts, passwords, access tokens and linked devices.
- The whole shop database: items, customers, suppliers, invoices, stock, treasury, shifts and reports.
- Uploaded files (logo, item images, import files).
- The shop's registration on the platform (subdomain and shop code).

## 4. Data that is retained, and why

- **The shop's platform subscription invoices and payment receipts:** kept for the period required by Egyptian tax and commercial law (up to `5` years), then deleted.
- **A short central audit record:** proves that the deletion happened, when and by whom, without any shop data content.
- **Encrypted backups:** expire automatically at the end of the retention cycle (up to `90` days) and are only used for emergency recovery before then.
- **The deletion request itself:** kept as evidence that the request was handled.

## 5. Important notes

- Deletion is final and cannot be undone once carried out. Export your data and reports first.
- An outstanding balance does not block deletion, but unpaid subscription invoices stay in the platform's records.
